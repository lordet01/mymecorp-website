Getting Started

  1. Download Kivo from the Mac App Store and launch the app.
  2. Sign in when prompted.
  3. Grant Microphone permission when macOS asks.
  4. Enable Accessibility permission for Kivo in System Settings.
  5. Open a text field (for example, TextEdit), place the cursor, then press and hold your Kivo shortcut while speaking.
  6. Release the shortcut, review the generated text, and finish any precise edits with your keyboard.

Kivo is a menu bar app. Look for the Kivo icon in the macOS menu bar to open settings, plans, and status.

Microphone Permission

Kivo needs microphone access to capture voice input when you activate push-to-talk. Recording starts only when you press the shortcut.

  • Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.
  • Find Kivo and turn it on.
  • If Kivo does not appear, launch the app once and try voice input again so macOS can prompt you.

Accessibility Permission

Accessibility permission lets Kivo insert generated text into the app that currently has keyboard focus.

  • Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  • Enable Kivo.
  • If text is not inserted after dictation, turn Kivo off and on again in that list, then restart Kivo.

Subscription and Billing

Kivo includes 10 free minutes. Personal and Pro subscriptions are available for higher usage limits and additional capabilities.

  • Open plans from Kivo menu bar icon → Settings → Plans.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled through your Apple Account settings.
  • Available plans and prices are shown in the app and may vary by country or region.
  • To manage or cancel a subscription: System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions (or Account settings on the device where you subscribed).

For billing questions that Apple handles (refunds, payment method, receipt), contact Apple Support. For product usage questions, email kivo@mymecorp.xyz.

Usage Limits

  • A usage warning appears at about 80% of your plan allowance.
  • Cloud processing stops when you reach 100% of the limit.
  • Upgrade your plan or wait for the next billing period to continue processing.

Troubleshooting

  • Shortcut does nothing: Confirm Kivo is running, check the shortcut in Settings, and make sure another app is not capturing the same keys.
  • No text appears: Place the cursor in a standard text field, verify Accessibility permission, and try TextEdit as a quick test.
  • “Listening” never starts: Check Microphone permission and that you are pressing/holding the configured shortcut.
  • Processing fails: Confirm your Mac is online. If you are near a usage limit, open Plans to review remaining minutes.
  • Still stuck: Email kivo@mymecorp.xyz with your macOS version, Kivo version, and a short description of the steps you tried.
