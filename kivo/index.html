FAQ

Common questions

Does Kivo replace my keyboard?

No. Speak the long draft, then use the keyboard for names, code, numbers, and final polish.

Which apps can I use it in?

Place the cursor in a supported text field — coding agents, chat, email, documents, notes, and other standard macOS text inputs — then activate Kivo.

Why does Kivo need Accessibility permission?

Accessibility permission lets Kivo insert generated text into the app that currently has focus.

What happens when I reach my usage limit?

You’ll see a warning at about 80% of your plan allowance. Cloud processing stops at 100%. Upgrade or wait for the next billing period to continue.
