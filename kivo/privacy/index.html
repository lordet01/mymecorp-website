Kivo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-28 · Operator: myme Corp. OÜ

This Privacy Policy describes how myme Corp. OÜ (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use Kivo, a voice typing app for macOS.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: email address and sign-in identifiers needed to create and manage your account
  • Voice input: audio you choose to capture when you activate push-to-talk, processed to generate text
  • Generated text & usage: transcription results and usage metrics needed to provide the service and enforce plan limits
  • Device & operations: basic device/app information and diagnostics needed for support, security, and reliability
  • Purchases: subscription and transaction identifiers processed through the Apple App Store

2. How we use data

  • Provide speech recognition and text processing
  • Insert generated text into the app you are using (with Accessibility permission)
  • Authenticate your account and manage Free / Personal / Pro plan limits
  • Customer support, fraud prevention, and legal compliance

3. Permissions on macOS

  • Microphone: required to capture voice input when you activate Kivo. Recording starts only when you press the shortcut.
  • Accessibility: required to place generated text into the currently focused application.
  • Internet: required for speech recognition and text processing.

4. Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: processes subscription payments and related transaction records.
  • We may use service providers that process data on our behalf under appropriate safeguards, solely to operate Kivo.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the security of the service and our users.

5. Retention & deletion

  • We retain account and usage data only as long as needed to provide the service, enforce plan limits, and meet legal obligations.
  • To request account and data deletion, email kivo@mymecorp.xyz or see Delete Account.
  • App Store subscriptions are managed separately in your Apple Account settings.

6. International transfers

Data may be processed on servers located outside your country. We apply appropriate safeguards as required.

7. Your rights

  • Request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal data
  • Contact us with privacy questions at kivo@mymecorp.xyz
