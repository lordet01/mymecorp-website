Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how myme ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use our AI portrait and ID photo services.

1. Data We Process

  • Uploaded image data (selfies, portraits, or other photos you submit for AI generation).
  • App usage data needed for service operation, fraud prevention, and performance monitoring.
  • Contact details you provide when reaching out for customer support.

2. How We Use Data

  • To generate AI portraits, ID photos, and related outputs requested by you.
  • To maintain service security, reliability, and quality.
  • To respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

3. Image Retention and Deletion

Uploaded image data is deleted immediately after AI generation is completed. In limited operational cases (for example, temporary processing failure handling), data may be retained briefly and is deleted no later than 24 hours after upload.

4. No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell personal data, including image data, to any third party.

5. Sharing with Service Providers

We may use trusted processors (for infrastructure, analytics, or customer support) strictly to operate the service under contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations.

6. GDPR Compliance (EU/EEA Users)

We process personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Where applicable, our legal bases include service performance, legitimate interests, and legal compliance.

EU/EEA users have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete their personal data.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

8. Children's Privacy

If a child photo is uploaded by a parent or legal guardian, the same protections in this policy apply. We do not knowingly sell or use child image data for unrelated marketing purposes.
