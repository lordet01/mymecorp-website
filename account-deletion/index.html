Account & Data Deletion

App: myme  |  Developer: myme Corp. OÜ

Last updated: 2026-06-08

Use this page to request deletion of your myme account and associated personal data. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the myme app and sign in with the same Google or Apple account you used to register. If you no longer have the app installed, skip to step 2.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account, review the notice, and confirm deletion. This starts the deletion request immediately while you are signed in.
  3. Alternatively, email us at myme@mymecorp.xyz with the subject line Account deletion request. Include the email address linked to your myme account and, if possible, the sign-in provider (Google or Apple).
  4. We verify your identity and process the request. You will receive a confirmation email when deletion is complete, usually within 30 days.

Active subscriptions: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel Google Play or App Store subscriptions. Cancel any active subscription in your store account settings before or after requesting deletion.

What data is deleted or retained

When your account deletion is processed, we handle data as follows:

Data type Action Retention period (if retained)
Account email and login provider identifiers (Google/Apple) Deleted —
Uploaded photos, AI generation parameters, task IDs, and output metadata Deleted —
FCM push notification token Deleted —
RevenueCat subscriber profile (email, purchase/subscription records, store transaction IDs) Deletion requested from RevenueCat Removed per our agreement; residual store billing records may remain with Apple/Google
Device, crash, performance, and analytics data (Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics, AdMob where applicable) Deleted or anonymized where possible Aggregated or de-identified analytics may persist in third-party systems per their policies
Payment and commerce records required by law (tax, accounting, fraud prevention) Retained in limited form Up to 7 years where required by applicable law
Support correspondence related to your deletion request Retained only as needed to fulfill the request Up to 90 days after deletion is confirmed, then deleted or anonymized
