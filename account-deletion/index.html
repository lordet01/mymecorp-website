Account & Data Deletion

App: myme studio  |  Developer: myme Corp. OÜ

Last updated: 2026-08-01

Use this page to request deletion of your myme studio account and associated personal data. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the myme studio app and sign in with the same Google or Apple account you used to register. If you no longer have the app installed, skip to step 2.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account, review the notice, and confirm deletion. This starts the deletion request immediately while you are signed in.
  3. Alternatively, email us at myme@mymecorp.xyz with the subject line Account deletion request. Include the email address linked to your myme studio account and, if possible, the sign-in provider (Google or Apple).
  4. We verify your identity and process the request. You will receive a confirmation email when deletion is complete, usually within 30 days.

Tokens: myme studio uses consumable token packages (one-time in-app purchases), not subscriptions—so there is nothing to cancel. Any remaining token balance is permanently removed when your account is deleted, and tokens are non-transferable and non-refundable outside the applicable store's refund policy. We recommend using any remaining tokens before requesting deletion.

What data is deleted or retained

When your account deletion is processed, we handle data as follows:

Data type Action Retention period (if retained)
Account email and login provider identifiers (Google/Apple) Deleted —
Face-containing photos, derived face analysis (e.g., frontal check, face region/size), and generation settings Deleted or anonymized —
Uploaded photos, AI generation parameters, task IDs, and output metadata Deleted or anonymized —

RevenueCat subscriber profile (email, token purchase records, store transaction IDs) Deletion requested from RevenueCat Removed per our agreement; residual store billing records may remain with Apple/Google

Payment and commerce records required by law (tax, accounting, fraud prevention) Retained in limited form Up to 7 years where required by applicable law
Support correspondence related to your deletion request Retained only as needed to fulfill the request Up to 90 days after deletion is confirmed, then deleted or anonymized
