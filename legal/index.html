Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. Where do I buy tokens?

Go to Settings → Token purchase and use Apple/Google in-app purchases.

Q2. I paid but my credits didn’t update.

Credits usually update within minutes via our purchase pipeline. If it’s delayed, email myme@mymecorp.xyz with the time of purchase and product name.

Q3. How do refunds work?

Digital goods may have restrictions depending on usage. See the Refund policy above and use the store refund flow when applicable.

Q4. Do I have to watch an ad to generate for free?

On the free (zero-token) path, generation starts after a rewarded ad. If the ad fails to load, the app may proceed depending on policy.

Q5. The result looks wrong / the face doesn’t match.

Results depend on lighting, pose, and image quality. Check the shooting guide for best practices.

Q6. What happens if I delete my account?

Account deletion removes your account, history, and creations where permitted; some records may be retained/anonymized where required by law. See our Account & Data Deletion page.

Q7. How do I stop marketing messages?

Turn off Marketing alerts in Settings (separate from system notification permission).

Q8. How do I know a photo is AI-generated?

When saving to the gallery, we inject AI-related metadata where supported (display may vary by platform/format).
