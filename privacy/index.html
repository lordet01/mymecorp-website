Privacy Policy for myme

Effective Date: June 2, 2026

myme Corp. OÜ ("we", "us", or "our") operates the myme mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our AI-powered ID and profile photo generation service, we collect the following types of information:

  • Image Data (Photos): When you use our service, we collect the facial images/selfies you upload. This is strictly required to process and generate your AI studio-quality photos.
  • Device and Usage Data: We automatically collect certain information about your device, such as your mobile device ID, operating system version, app usage statistics, and diagnostic data to improve app performance and stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service: Specifically, applying Artificial Intelligence algorithms to your uploaded images to generate standard ID or profile photos.
  • To improve the App: Analyzing usage data to fix bugs and enhance the user experience.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries and provide technical assistance.

3. Image Data Processing and Retention

We take your privacy seriously, especially regarding your photos:

  • Processing: Your photos are uploaded securely to our servers solely for the purpose of AI processing.
  • Retention: Uploaded photos and the generated AI photos are stored only temporarily. They are automatically deleted from our servers within 24 hours after the photo generation process is complete. We do not use your personal images to train our public AI models without your explicit consent.

4. Data Deletion Policy

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data.

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your generated photos and account history directly within the App by navigating to Settings.
  • Manual Request: You can also request the deletion of your data by contacting us at myme.corp.ou@gmail.com. We will process your request within 14 days.

These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

The security of your data is important to us. We use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal information. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13 (or 16 depending on your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
