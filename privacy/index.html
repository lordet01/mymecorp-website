myme Privacy Policy (Summary)

Last updated: 2026-06-08

1. Data we collect

  • Account: email and login provider identifiers (e.g., Google/Apple)
  • Service usage: uploaded photos, generation parameters, task IDs, output metadata
  • Device & operations: device information, diagnostics/crash/performance data, advertising/analytics identifiers (as permitted by your choices)
  • Notifications: FCM token (for push delivery)

2. Purposes

  • Provide AI portrait generation and improve quality
  • Customer support, fraud prevention, legal compliance
  • Marketing push is only sent if you opt in separately from system notifications.

3. Retention & deletion

  • When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize personal data where permitted.
  • Some records may be retained where required by law (e.g., tax/commerce).

4. Processors & third parties

  • Payments, subscriptions & in-app purchases
    • Apple App Store, Google Play: store payment processing
    • RevenueCat (subscription/IAP management and receipt validation)
      • Data shared: your account email address (used as the App User ID), purchase and subscription records, store transaction identifiers
      • When shared: immediately after a successful Google/Apple sign-in, and on subsequent app launches while signed in
      • Purpose: sync in-app products and subscription status, restore purchases, manage billing history, and prevent fraud
      • Retention & deletion: governed by RevenueCat’s privacy policy and our agreement. We request deletion of your RevenueCat subscriber data when you delete your account.
  • Ads: Google AdMob (consent flows such as UMP/ATT apply)
  • Analytics/crash reporting: Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics (may be disabled depending on build settings)

5. International transfers

Data may be processed on servers located outside your country; we apply appropriate safeguards as required.

6. Your rights

  • Request access, correction, deletion, or restriction (contact support)
  • Withdraw marketing consent anytime in Settings
