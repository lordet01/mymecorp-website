myme Privacy Policy (Summary)

Last updated: 2026-06-23

1. Data we collect

  • Account: email and login provider identifiers (e.g., Google/Apple)
  • Face data & photos: photos you upload or capture that contain your face; derived analysis (e.g., face position, frontal check, face count/size). We do not collect biometric templates for device unlock or Face ID–style authentication.
  • Service usage: uploaded photos, generation parameters, task IDs, output metadata
  • Device & operations: device information, diagnostics/crash/performance data, advertising/analytics identifiers (as permitted by your choices)
  • Notifications: FCM token (for push delivery)

Face data — what we collect, use, share, and retain

What face data we collect

  • Photos containing your face that you choose to upload, import, or capture in the app
  • Derived face-related analysis results (e.g., frontal check, approximate face region/size, face count) needed to provide the service
  • Generation settings you select (style, background, retouch level, etc.)

How we use face data

  • Generate AI portrait, ID, and profile photos
  • Check photo quality and composition before generation
  • Improve service quality and prevent abuse

Server transmission & storage

  • To provide app features such as AI analysis and image generation, face data is transmitted to and processed on secure servers operated by myme Corp. OÜ
  • Data may be stored on cloud servers outside your country (see Section 5)
  • We do not sell face data

Retention

  • When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize face data and related photos where permitted
  • Some records may be retained where required by law (e.g., tax/commerce) — see Section 3

In-app consent

Before your first photo upload or AI generation, the app explains what is sent, who receives it, and asks for your permission. Privacy Policy alone is not sufficient; consent is requested in the app.

2. Purposes

  • Provide AI portrait generation and improve quality
  • To provide app features such as AI analysis and image generation, your data is transmitted to and processed on servers operated by myme Corp. OÜ
  • Customer support, fraud prevention, legal compliance
  • Marketing push is only sent if you opt in separately from system notifications.

3. Retention & deletion

  • When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize personal data where permitted.
  • Some records may be retained where required by law (e.g., tax/commerce).

4. Processors & third parties

  • AI image analysis & generation
    • Processor & location: secure cloud servers operated directly by myme Corp. OÜ (our proprietary image-generation AI model)
    • Data transmitted & processed: face-containing photos, analysis outputs, generation parameters, task/session identifiers
    • When: after you agree in the in-app consent dialog, when you upload/analyze photos, or request AI generation
    • Purpose: portrait generation, quality validation, service improvement, fraud/abuse prevention
    • Protection: contractual and technical safeguards consistent with this policy
    • Note on AI Features: Our app does NOT use any third-party AI services. All AI processing is done using our proprietary image-generation AI model hosted entirely on our own secure servers. No user data is shared with external AI providers.
  • Payments, subscriptions & in-app purchases
    • Apple App Store, Google Play: store payment processing
    • RevenueCat (subscription/IAP management and receipt validation)
      • Data shared: your account email address (used as the App User ID), purchase and subscription records, store transaction identifiers
      • When shared: immediately after a successful Google/Apple sign-in, and on subsequent app launches while signed in
      • Purpose: sync in-app products and subscription status, restore purchases, manage billing history, and prevent fraud
      • Retention & deletion: governed by RevenueCat’s privacy policy and our agreement. We request deletion of your RevenueCat subscriber data when you delete your account.
  • Ads: Google AdMob (consent flows such as UMP/ATT apply)
  • Analytics/crash reporting: Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics (may be disabled depending on build settings)

5. International transfers

Data may be processed on servers located outside your country; we apply appropriate safeguards as required.

6. Your rights

  • Request access, correction, deletion, or restriction (contact support)
  • Withdraw marketing consent anytime in Settings
